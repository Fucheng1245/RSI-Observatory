# Start Here

RSI Observatory helps readers compare self-improving AI systems, inspect the evidence behind their claims, and follow new research.

[← Observatory](README.md) · [Methodology](METHODOLOGY.md) · [Research library](PAPERS.md)

## A five-minute tour

1. **Understand the boundaries.** Read the [Landscape](LANDSCAPE.md): what changes, how recursive the loop is, and what evidence supports it are separate questions.
2. **Compare systems.** Open the [Scorecard](SCORECARD.md). Read each rationale alongside its label; a classification is an editorial assessment, not independent replication.
3. **Choose an evaluation.** Use the [Benchmark guide](BENCHMARKS.md) to distinguish retained experience, method improvement, and broader research capability.
4. **Find a reading path.** The [Research library](PAPERS.md) includes a 20-work introduction and an index grouped by research area.
5. **Follow updates.** Read the [latest Radar](radar/LATEST.md). Repository popularity measures attention, not capability.

## Reading the labels

**Core** means self-improvement is central to a research work; it does not automatically mean recursive self-improvement. L-levels describe recursive depth. E-levels describe evidence under the Observatory's editorial framework. Consult the [Methodology](METHODOLOGY.md) before comparing labels.

A dash in the seven-day growth column means an appropriate baseline is unavailable. Automated paper discovery is separate from curated inclusion.
